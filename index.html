Export Data

Export your categorized transactions and rules for QuickBooks Desktop and other formats.

Chart of Accounts (IIF)

Exports the accounts referenced by your categorized transactions, including inferred QuickBooks account types.

Journal Entries (IIF)

Exports categorized transactions as journal entries. Provide the bank account name used on the TRNS line.

QuickBooks Desktop (.qbo)

Exports transactions to a .qbo file (Web Connect) for easy import into QuickBooks Desktop.

Classified Transactions (Excel)

Exports all categorized transactions to an Excel file, including Date, Description, Amount, and Schedule C Category.

Classification Rules (CSV)

Exports the currently loaded rules that matched your transactions, including match statistics.

QBO Bank Rules (Excel)

Exports loaded rules in QuickBooks Online import format (.xls). Rule names are automatically generated from keywords.
